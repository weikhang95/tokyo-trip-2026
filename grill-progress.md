# Itinerary grill: progress

Hand-off note for continuing on another machine. Started 2026-09-27. Each stop is being checked against one question: is it something we actually want, or is it only there because guides call it a must-do? The handbook is `index.html`; `companion.html` mirrors it.

## Ground rules (agreed)

- The trip is weighted **60% toward Mom and 40% toward me**. A stop stays only if you can say in one line what you'll do there and it meets at least one of these:
  - Mom will genuinely enjoy it.
  - I really want to shoot or eat it (at most one of these a day).
  - It is on the way, a rest stop, or a meal.
- Mom is easygoing and wants to try a bit of everything: scenery, temples, food, shopping, sitting and watching. The one category we skip is "cute little things".
- **Mom eats no raw food.** She does eat cooked eel and fish. Every shared meal needs a cooked dish for her. Raw-only shops are an optional extra for me.
